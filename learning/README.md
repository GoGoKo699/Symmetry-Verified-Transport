# Learning the project

A number check can confirm that a container still holds two particles without confirming that either particle stayed in the right place. This project makes that distinction exact for a noisy spin chain, then asks whether the information in the retained runs pays for the rejected ones.

The selected educational anchor is **John Watrous, _The Theory of Quantum Information_ (Cambridge University Press, 2018)**. Its language of states, channels, measurement records and distinguishability provides the foundation. The transport, conditional-kernel and cadence calculations are supplied in this repository.

The map has **12 background milestones, M01–M12, and 18 narrative milestones, M13–M30**. The background moves from one occupation bit to the complete observation and resource contract. The narrative builds the conditional channel, combines checks, compares complete records and raw inference, and finishes with the result's boundaries and attribution.

The bridge follows the same story through four spins. It explains why acceptance, retained visibility and information per initialized attempt are separate quantities. The milestone map supplies dependencies and source pointers for deeper reading; the scientific chapters retain the complete arguments.
